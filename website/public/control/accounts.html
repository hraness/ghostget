Sign in in your browser, then return here to verify the account. Ghostget installs its bundled capabilities if none are installed. A saved locator is not proof of a current session.

Import an X token from 1Password

Choose a specific 1Password field for the supported X token import. Only the private credential helper resolves it. Ghostget keeps a local copy of the imported token until you disconnect it or revoke it at X. Locking 1Password does not revoke this copy. Password and passkey sign-in continues in your browser.
